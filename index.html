FAQs

How do I get to the hotel upon arrival?

We will be putting you guys in travel groups based on your arrival times. Information on that will follow via Whatsapp.

Arrival in Larnaca/Paphos on 6th of August: We strongly recommend you to not book any hotels by yourself, instead we will transport you directly to the wedding hotel upon arrival.
Reason for that is because on the 7th we are already fully busy with transferring friends and family from the airport, so we will not have time to transfer other groups.
Upon arrival you will have the take the Kapnos Airport Shuttle from the airport to Nicosia. Departure times can be found online at https://kapnosairportshuttle.com/timetables. You can book the tickets online or in person at the Kapnos ticket booth upon arrival. The price is €10 from Larnaca and €18 from Paphos airport. Buses from Larnaca go almost every hour. Buses from Paphos have very specific times, so keep that in mind!
We will pick you up from the Nicosia Kapnos station and transport you over the border and bring you directly to the Hotel.

Arrival in Larnaca/Paphos on 7th of August: You will have to the take the Kapnos Airport Shuttle from the airport to Nicosia. Departure times can be found online at https://kapnosairportshuttle.com/timetables. You can book the tickets online or in person at the Kapnos ticket booth upon arrival. The price is €10 from Larnaca and €18 from Paphos. Buses from Larnaca go almost every hour. Buses from Paphos have very specific times, so keep that in mind!
We will pick you up from the Nicosia Kapnos station and transport you over the border. We will either bring you directly to the hotel, or drop you off in Kyrenia, depending on our available time. From there you can either stay in Kyrenia (for the special event in the evening) or take a taxi to the hotel.
If your flight arrival is very early in the morning on the 7th, then we also recommend you to take the Kapnos Airport shuttle to Nicosia and then take a taxi to Mariyam's home. You can spend the night there and then we will drive you to the hotel/Kyrenia on the 7th of August.

Arrival in Ercan: You can take a bus to Kyrenia and then a taxi to the hotel from there. Alternatively you can take a taxi directly to the hotel. In special cases we might be able to pick you up, but if that's the case we will contact you privately.

What hotel will we be staying in?

Our wedding guests will be staying at the Lapida Garden Hotel. You can find more information about the hotel here: https://www.lapidahotel.com/


Price per room for 2 persons is €55, plus extra €10 for every extra bed. Breakfast included.


The hotel is a small, family-style hotel with around 30 rooms. All rooms have been reserved for our wedding guests. There are rooms for two, three, and four people. Room allocation will be arranged by us. When you arrive at the hotel, you will receive your room number and (if relevant) you can pay for your room directly at check-in. Card payment is possible. If you prefer to pay in cash, Turkish Lira is best.

Check-in: 14:00 (you can come earlier and leave your stuff at the hotel/chill at the pool if your room isn't ready)
Check-out: 12:00 (you can leave your stuff at the hotel/chill at the pool if you have a later flight)

All rooms are equipped with air conditioning and a fridge. Most rooms also have a balcony. Shampoo, shower gel, hairdryer and towels are also provided.

Breakfast is served as a buffet from 8:30 to 10:30. For lunch and dinner, you can either eat directly at the hotel from a small menu, have food delivered to the pool area or visit one of the nearby restaurants (we recommend Driftfood). The hotel also has a garden-style pool area with a pool bar. Since we are the only guests, the opening hours of the bar and reception may be flexible. They will likely stay open as long as people are still ordering drinks.

Wi-Fi is available at the hotel. During our last visit, it was not working very well, but the hotel promised to get it fixed. If you need to rely on a stable internet connection, we recommend getting an eSIM just to be safe.

Arrival day for all guests at the hotel. In the evening, we have organized a special event for everyone in Girne / Kyrenia Harbour. The event will start at 18:30. Please arrive on time, if you are late, you won't be able to participate. The meeting point is in front of PUSULA Fish Restaurant.


For people going to the harbour directly from the hotel, we will organize a transfer/taxis all together. The drive from the hotel to the harbour takes around 30 minutes.

To bring with you to the event: swimwear, a towel, sunglasses, sun protection, some cash or a credit card, and of course good mood. We decided not to provide food during this event. We recommend eating at the beautiful harbour either before the event (but don't eat too much) or after. The event will end around 21:30 to 22:30, so you can also have late dinner there after the event. For the people going directly from the hotel we can order some pizza to the hotel before driving to the event.

Feel free to do whatever you like and organize groups with each other, just be back at the hotel and ready to go on time for the main event.

What is around the hotel?

The hotel is located between the sea and the mountains in a little holiday seaside area. The nearest beach is around 15 minutes away on foot. Please note that many beaches in North Cyprus are private and belong to restaurants. This means you can often combine going for food or drinks with swimming in the sea. Please either walk to the beach or organize taxis together with other guests. The hotel staff will be happy to help you with this. Parking at the hotel and near all restaurants is available for free in case you decide to rent a car.


Next to the hotel, there is a small minimarket where you can buy water, snacks, and other basic items. Please note that tap water in all of Cyprus is not drinkable, so you should buy bottled water.


5 Minutes walk from the hotel is also a stunning walking area right next to the sea that passes through a little fishing port. If you want to go for a sunset walk with the sea (literally) right beneath you, we recommend going there. You just have to walk directly down to the sea from the hotel.

How do we get to the wedding venue on 8th of August?

The wedding venue is around 35 minutes away on foot or around 5 minutes by car from the hotel. Transport to the venue and back will be organized by us (either shuttle busses or taxis).

What can I do in Northern Cyprus?

- St. Hilarion Castle
- Bellapais Castle
- Varosha (ghost town)
- Kyrenia Harbour (we recommend visiting here on Friday midday and having early dinner in one of the tavernas, since this is the meeting point for our special even later Friday evening)
- Nicosia Center Old town (We recommend seeing this before check-in or after check-out, as it is around 40 minutes away from the hotel and right next to the Southern side)
- Karpaz Area with the abandoned Churches and wild beaches (we will be very surprised if you manage to find the time and the means of transport to geth there haha)
- Beaches near the hotel: Vasilia Beach (wide beach with nice views, with turtle nests, a bit on the "natury" side), Escape Beach (water sports and other activities available), Driftfood Beach (convenient location with sandy beach) If anyone is interested in hiking in the nearby mountains, please contact us.
